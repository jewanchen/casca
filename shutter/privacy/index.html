中文

Casca Shutter(瀏覽器擴充與本機程式)在你的電腦上運作。它不向任何伺服器傳送你的內容、個人資料或使用紀錄。

  • 偵測與遮蔽在你的電腦內記憶體中進行;你送出的文字、AI 的回答、對照表都只存在這台電腦。
  • 擴充唯一的網路連線對象是同一台電腦上的 Casca Shutter 本機程式(127.0.0.1),用於顯示狀態、暫停/關閉控制與「型別 × 次數」統計;統計不含任何內容。
  • 本機程式唯一的對外連線是每日一次的更新檢查(可關閉);該請求不含任何識別資訊。
  • 我們不蒐集、不儲存、不販售、不分享任何使用者資料。
  • 如你的公司以受管模式部署,政策與型別統計報表由你的公司 IT 管理,內容仍不會被記錄。

English

Casca Shutter (browser extension and local agent) runs on your computer. It does not send your content, personal data or usage records to any server.

  • Detection and masking happen in memory on your computer; the text you send, the AI's replies and the placeholder map exist only on this device.
  • The extension's only network connection is to the Casca Shutter local agent on the same computer (127.0.0.1), used for status, pause/off controls and "type × count" statistics that never include content.
  • The local agent's only outbound connection is a once-a-day update check (can be disabled); the request carries no identifying information.
  • We do not collect, store, sell or share any user data.
  • If your organisation deploys managed mode, policy and type-count reports are administered by your IT; content is still never recorded.
