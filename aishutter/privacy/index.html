English

Casca AIShutter (browser extension and local agent) runs on your computer. It does not send your content, personal data or usage records to any server.

  • The extension runs only on these AI sites: chat.deepseek.com, chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com, claude.ai, gemini.google.com, copilot.microsoft.com and copilot.com. Without the local program it still works on its own inside the browser; your pause, off and excluded-site choices are stored locally in the browser.
  • Detection and masking happen in memory on your computer; the text you send, the AI's replies and the placeholder map exist only on this device.
  • The extension's only network connection is to the Casca AIShutter local agent on the same computer (127.0.0.1), used for status, pause/off controls and "type × count" statistics that never include content.
  • The local agent's only outbound connection is a once-a-day update check (can be disabled); the request carries no identifying information.
  • We do not collect, store, sell or share any user data.
  • If your organisation deploys managed mode, policy and type-count reports are administered by your IT; content is still never recorded.
